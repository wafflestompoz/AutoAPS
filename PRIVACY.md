# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-01_

AutoAPS does not collect, store or share any personal or health data with its developers or with anyone else.

- Your Nightscout address and access token are stored only on your phone, in app-private storage. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.
- The app connects only to the Nightscout address you enter, using read-only requests. Glucose, insulin and carbohydrate data is fetched from your site and shown on your car display; it is kept in memory only and is not written to disk.
- AutoAPS contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs that send data anywhere.
- Your Nightscout site's own privacy depends on how you host it; that is outside AutoAPS.

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
