Home screen widget

The widget comes in three sizes and shows the same scenario as the car screen above. Google is bringing phone widgets to the Android Auto dashboard, so this same widget is meant to appear in the car once that reaches your phone. It refreshes about every 5 minutes, and tapping it opens setup.

Important: read before use

AutoAPS is not a medical device. It shows a copy of your Nightscout data, which can be delayed or wrong.
  • Never make treatment decisions from AutoAPS.
  • It has no alarms. Keep CGM alerts on.
  • Don't use or set it up while driving.

What you're looking at

  • One screen, three rows. The title and row count stay fixed, so Android Auto treats each 60-second update as a refresh. That keeps the app clear of the car's limit on screen changes.
  • Stale readings. After 15 minutes without new data, the trend arrow and delta are hidden and the reading is marked STALE. Press "Wait 5 minutes" a few times to see it.
  • LOW and HIGH labels appear below 70 and above 180 mg/dL. The app has no alarms on purpose.
  • Errors keep the last reading. If Nightscout can't be reached, the last good values stay on screen with their age, and the reason appears on the bottom row.
